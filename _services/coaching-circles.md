# Coaching Circles

A Coaching Circle is a small cohort that meet with a coach on a regular cadence to build relationships while discussing challenges, leadership development opportunities, and practicing concepts introduced in the group. A Circle is also a space where members can get support or coaching on any challenges they are facing from each other.

## :star: The Why

Company, organization, or industry boundaries can make it difficult to connect with others, learn new perspectives, and to feel supported in our learning and growth. A Circle breaks down those barriers, eliminates the power gradients we might experience with learning within our organizations, and allows us to meet new people outside of our immediate surroundings.

A Circle is fun, purpose-driven, interactive and creates a lasting connection and community with participants.

The participants are fully supported by and receive encouragement from a coach and their peers, as well as professional insight, methodologies and a framework for every step of the way.

## :star: The How

A Circle is supported and facilitated by me, a trained and certified coach.

Some characteristics/behaviors you can expect to see in a Circle:
* Vulnerability without the fear of needing to know the answers.
* Curiosity without needing to give the answers to others.
* Respectful candidness over artificial harmony.
* Growth mindset (“what is possible?”) and open to possibilities.
* Most importantly, lasting and meaningful relationships (that last well beyond sessions)

### Structure of a Circle

**Session 1: Getting to know each other.** 
Circle members and coach(es) introduce themselves, share what they are hoping to get out of the Circle. By the end of session 1, the Circle will have a set of shared agreements and a set of goals that members are hoping to get out of participating.

**Sessions 2+: Supporting, coaching, and learning from each other.**
 Each session will begin with participants sharing their progress or insights between previous sessions and choose a topic to explore for the current session.

Each session is participant-led, with support from a coach. Throughout the discussion, a coach will help participants reframe challenges that arise to see them in a new light, as indications of your opportunities for continued growth. A coach will help participants to identify blind spots and overcome obstacles.

Sessions can leverage different formats based on the needs of the Circle. Some examples are:
- a group discussion with topics chosen by the Circle
- peer coaching or coaching practice
- practicing a new topic, framework, or concept together

The total number of live sessions supported by a coach will be determined by the group; although the intention is a circle can continue to support and learn from each other indefinitely.
