# Individual Coaching

As a certified professional coach, I work with individuals to:

- Discover and align with their core values and purpose
- Develop effective leadership skills
- Navigate career transitions
- Build stronger relationships and communication
- Create sustainable organizational change
- Working through difficult situations
- Navigating conflict

Each coaching relationship is tailored for you, the client, where we focus on your goals and the actions you will take to achieve them.

## Coaching Relationship

A typical coaching engagement includes:

- **Introduction and Fit Meeting -** We will learn more about your interest in coaching, gain a shared understanding on what to expect from each of us, and share my approach and how I will work with you.
- **Coaching Agreement -** If we both believe there is a fit and choose to continue after the introductory session, we will create a shared coaching contract that includes a set of agreements and expectations on what you hope to achieve from of the coaching engagement.
- **Coaching sessions -** We begin meeting for a set number of coaching sessions, scheduled on a cadence (typically twice a month). Most coaching engagements are between 10 - 12 sessions over a 6 month period.

## Ethical Coaching

As an ICF-certified coach (ACC), I firmly believe in and follow the [ICF Code of Ethics](https://coachingfederation.org/ethics/code-of-ethics).
